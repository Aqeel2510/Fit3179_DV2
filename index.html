Where Are the big spenders?

Which States Spend the Most?

New South Wales leads Australia's online spending market,
recording $26.4 billion in 2025.

Which States Spend More per Resident?

Queensland records the highest online expenditure
per resident, averaging $3,139 per person.

Does Online Spending Match Population?

Spending shares differ from population shares,
with Victoria showing the largest gap.

DATA DOCUMENTATION

Metadata & Sources

This dashboard combines publicly available Australian statistical data and eCommerce industry research to explore online shopping trends, geographical differences, consumer preferences and purchasing behaviour.

Data Sources

Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)

Australian Statistical Geography Standard (ASGS) Digital Boundary Files - geographic boundary data for Australian states and territories, used to construct the choropleth, proportional symbol and Dorling cartogram maps.

Retail Trade, Australia - historical monthly online retail turnover, including the experimental online retail turnover series.

National, State and Territory Population - estimated resident population used for geographical and per-resident comparisons.

Australia Post

2026 eCommerce Report - online shopping expenditure, purchasing behaviour, categories, generations and geographical shopping trends.

July 2026 Quarterly eCommerce Update - supplementary insights on Australia's online shopping market.
